I am an undergraduate student at the School of Art & Technology, Chung-Ang University. My research focuses on trustworthy and safe AI, with an emphasis on understanding when, where, and how Large Language Models (LLMs) fail before those failures appear in their final outputs.

## Research Interests

- LLM safety failure observability
- Jailbreak attacks and refusal behavior
- Trustworthy AI systems and evaluation
- RAG-based systems for domain-specific reasoning

## Current Work

My current work studies how safety failures emerge during generation, rather than only after a final response is produced. In [Beyond Attack Success Rate: Temporal Logit Observability for LLM Safety Failures](/publication/2026-01-22-beyond-attack-success-rate-temporal-logit-observability-llm-safety-failures), I examine generation-time evidence such as logit dynamics, refusal and compliance margins, early-token behavior, and evaluation signals that reveal how safety failures form during decoding.

## Selected Work

- [Beyond Attack Success Rate: Temporal Logit Observability for LLM Safety Failures](/publication/2026-01-22-beyond-attack-success-rate-temporal-logit-observability-llm-safety-failures) studies generation-time signals for observing LLM safety failures before they appear in final outputs.
- [Persona Attack: Incremental Memory Injection Jailbreak Attack against Large Language Models](/publication/2025-05-01-persona-attack-incremental-memory-injection-jailbreak) investigates context-window-based jailbreak behavior through incremental memory injection.
- [A GraphRAG-Based Framework for Interpreting Financial Security Regulations](/publication/2025-11-01-graphrag-financial-security-regulation-interpretation-framework) applies GraphRAG to financial security regulation interpretation and was presented at the Korea Society for Electronic Commerce Fall Conference as a second-author work.

## Technical Background

My technical background includes Python, PyTorch, HuggingFace, RAG-style systems, jailbreak evaluation, LLM-as-Judge evaluation, LlamaGuard, HarmBench, GPT-4o-mini, and human labeling.
