I am an undergraduate student in the University of Art & Technology at Chung-Ang University. My research focuses on trustworthy AI and safe AI, especially on understanding when, where, and how Large Language Models(LLMs) fail before those failures appear in their final outputs.

My current work studies failure observability for large language models. In "Beyond Attack Success Rate: Temporal Logit Observability for LLM Safety Failures," submitted to NeurIPS 2026 Main, I examine generation-time evidence such as logit dynamics, refusal and compliance margins, early-token behavior, and evaluation signals that reveal how safety failures form during decoding.

I have also worked on context-window-based jailbreak behavior through "Persona Attack: Incremental Memory Injection Jailbreak Attack against Large Language Models," and on a GraphRAG-based framework for interpreting financial security regulations, presented at the Korea Society for Electronic Commerce Fall Conference as a second-author work. My technical background includes Python, PyTorch, HuggingFace, RAG-style systems, jailbreak evaluation, LLM-as-Judge evaluation, LlamaGuard, HarmBench, GPT-4o-mini, and human labeling.
